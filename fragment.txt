The morning began quietly in the small town by the sea. Fishermen were already on the water, and the gulls circled above the harbor.
Anna walked slowly along the old stone road, carrying a basket of bread and fresh apples.

The road was long, but she knew every turn by heart. Every house had a story, and every window had a face she knew.
The baker waved from his door, and the children ran past her laughing.

By noon the sun was high and warm. Anna sat under a tree and listened to the sea. The sea was calm, and the town was calm, and for a moment she wished the day would never end.
